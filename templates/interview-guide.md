# Customer Interview Guide — Positioning Research

## Protocol
Obtain consent; explain note use; ask about actual past behavior; ask unaided questions before showing concepts; avoid leading questions; record role, date, and recruitment method; separate verbatim observations from interpretation.

## Opening
1. Tell me about your role and decisions in this area.
2. When did this need last come up?
3. What triggered it?

## Choice reconstruction
4. What options did you consider?
5. Which options did you reject first, and why?
6. What did you compare?
7. What information or evidence mattered most?
8. Who else influenced the decision?
9. What nearly stopped the purchase?
10. What would make you switch?

## Category and brand language
11. What would you call this type of product/service?
12. Which brands come to mind first? (Unaided)
13. What do you associate with each brand?
14. What is each brand especially good or bad at?
15. Which brand would you recommend, and in what situation?

## Trust and proof
16. Which claims do you believe?
17. Which claims sound like advertising?
18. What proof would change your mind?
19. What would make a new entrant credible?

## Closing
20. If your current solution disappeared tomorrow, what would you do?
21. What have I not asked that matters to this decision?
