# Positioning Statement

## Target audience
Who is the specific audience? Include buyer/user roles and exclusions.

## Buying situation / customer need
What triggers the choice? What job is the customer trying to get done?

## Competitive frame
What category or alternatives does the customer use to compare options?

## Point of difference
What meaningful difference should the audience associate with this offer?

## Reasons to believe
List product facts, performance data, service capability, proof, certification, or customer evidence. Mark source and grade.

## Internal positioning statement
For [target audience] who [need / buying situation], [brand / offer] is the [competitive frame] that [meaningful point of difference], because [reasons to believe].
